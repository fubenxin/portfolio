FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 50 pcs per model. Sample orders of 1–5 pcs are accepted for quality testing.

Can I get a sample before placing an order?

Yes. Samples are available in 3–7 days. Sample cost can be refunded once you place a bulk order.

What payment terms do you accept?

T/T (30% deposit, 70% before shipment), L/C at sight, PayPal and Western Union for small orders.

How long is the delivery time?

Usually 15–25 days after deposit for bulk orders, depending on quantity and customization.

Do you provide OEM service?

Yes. We offer custom logo, packaging, specifications and fully new product development.
